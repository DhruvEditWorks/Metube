MeTube — How to Change & Replace Side Video Thumbnails
========================================================================

All playlist and lesson thumbnails are stored in this folder:
`assets/thumbnails/`

The current sample stock images in this folder are:
- `thumb_lesson1.jpg` -> Thumbnail for Lesson 1 (The Language of Silence)
- `thumb_lesson2.jpg` -> Thumbnail for Lesson 2 (Greetings Without Sound)
- `thumb_lesson3.jpg` -> Thumbnail for Lesson 3 (Family in Fingertips)
- `thumb_lesson4.jpg` -> Thumbnail for Lesson 4 (Emotions in Gestures)
- `thumb_lesson5.jpg` -> Thumbnail for Lesson 5 (Daily Life in Signs)
- `thumb_lesson6.jpg` -> Thumbnail for Lesson 6 (Advanced Conversations)
- `main_poster.jpg`   -> Main video player poster cover

------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO REPLACE WITH YOUR OWN IMAGES:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Simply save any image you want to use in this folder with the same name
   (e.g., `thumb_lesson1.jpg`, `thumb_lesson2.jpg`, etc.).
2. Supported formats: JPG, PNG, WEBP (16:9 ratio, recommended 640x360 or 1280x720).
3. Refresh `index.html` in your browser to see your updated custom thumbnails instantly!
